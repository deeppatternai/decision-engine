# Decision Engine v0.2.106

## Added

- Added decimal GE Chat pricing support so fractional credit amounts are validated, displayed, and preserved across the popup request lifecycle.

## Changed

- Authenticated GE Chat requests now advertise the decimal pricing contract and parse numeric prices without an intermediate binary floating-point conversion.

## Fixed

- Preserved fractional prices exactly through the HTTP client, native bridge, and browser UI while rejecting negative, over-precision, non-finite, and unsafe-range values.

## Known limitations

- Decimal GE Chat prices are bounded by the client wire contract and remain subject to the service's credit and quota policies.
- Prices support at most six fractional digits and must remain within the browser-safe micro-unit range.
